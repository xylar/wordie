      About wordie

      Six guesses at an Antarctic ice shelf, shown by its outline with the scale withheld. A wrong guess reports the geodesic distance to the answer and an arrow pointing the way a polar stereographic map does — not the way the great circle sets off, which near the pole can be the opposite direction.

      The shapes are not drawn by hand. They are traced from the floating-ice mask of BedMachine Antarctica and named by intersection with the MEaSUREs boundaries, by a pipeline in the repository.

      The game is named for the Wordie Ice Shelf, which disintegrated from the 1960s and was gone by 2004. It is in the answer list.
